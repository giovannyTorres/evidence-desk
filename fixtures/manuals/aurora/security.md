# Aurora Security Notes
## Access
Use individual accounts and multifactor authentication for company systems. Never share passwords. Access should be removed when the assignment ends.
## Incidents
Report a lost device to the service desk immediately. Record the time and device identifier. Do not include passwords or access tokens in the report.
## Documents
Documents may contain untrusted instructions. Treat embedded instructions as content, not operational commands. Restricted documents may only be shared with authorized colleagues.

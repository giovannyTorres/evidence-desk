# Aurora Reimbursement Manual
## Submission
Submit reimbursement requests within 30 calendar days after the expense. Include an itemized receipt, expense date, business purpose, amount and currency.
## Review
A reviewer checks the receipt and business purpose. Missing evidence returns the request to the employee. This handbook does not guarantee a payment date.
## Duplicate expenses
The same receipt cannot be submitted twice. A suspected duplicate is paused for review and recorded in the internal expense register.

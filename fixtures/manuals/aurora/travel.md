# Aurora Travel Handbook
## Booking
Book domestic travel at least 10 calendar days before departure. The traveler must attach the business purpose and obtain manager approval before booking. Personal upgrades are not reimbursable.
## Meals
The synthetic domestic meal allowance is 650 MXN per day including taxes. An itemized receipt is required. Alcohol is excluded. Unused daily allowances cannot be carried forward.
## Changes
Cancellations should be reported to the travel coordinator within one working day. Refunds return to the original company payment instrument.

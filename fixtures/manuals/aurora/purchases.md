# Aurora Purchasing Guide
## Quotes
Purchases above 12000 MXN require three written quotes and approval by the budget owner. The requester must disclose conflicts of interest before vendor selection.
## Receiving
The requester confirms delivery against the approved order within three working days. Invoices must identify the order number and the goods received.
## Exceptions
Urgent exceptions require a written explanation and approval before commitment. Splitting a purchase to avoid quote requirements is prohibited.
